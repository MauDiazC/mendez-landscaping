## Brand & Style

The brand represents an elevated, architectural approach to landscaping, arboriculture, and outdoor living. Rooted in organic luxury, it speaks to discerning homeowners, estate managers, and boutique commercial developers seeking bespoke outdoor transformations. The emotional resonance blends grounded tranquility with unquestioned expertise: clean, pristine craftsmanship balanced by raw botanical vitality.

The visual style marries **Minimalism** with **Modern Tactile Naturalism**:
- Deep botanical pigments anchored by crisp, warm natural substrates.
- Architectural typographic contrast featuring a refined, geometric contemporary sans-serif paired with sharp, highly legible structural scales.
- High visual craft characterized by spacious breathing room, pill-shaped tactile controls, and soft atmospheric depth over rigid material planes.
- Confidence expressed through clean before-and-after interactive comparisons, transparent process metrics, and effortless booking workflows.

## Colors

The color palette is built around deep organic greens and warm earthy foundations, steering clear of artificial neon lime hues in favor of natural botanic depth.

- **Primary (`#183d28`)**: Deep forest pine used for high-impact brand anchors, dark hero banners, primary buttons, footers, and prominent heading accents.
- **Secondary (`#2e7d45`)**: Living emerald green serving as an active interactive state, hover highlight, micro-badge icon container, and rating fill.
- **Tertiary (`#d8e8dc`)**: Soft sage green used for pill badge backgrounds, subtle callout borders, input focus tints, and light tag backdrops.
- **Neutral (`#18221c`)**: Deep botanical slate used for primary body text, headlines on light backgrounds, and high-contrast structural dividers.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base**: `#faf8f5` (Warm Cream Canvas) provides an organic, tactile paper-like foundation.
- **Card Surface**: `#ffffff` (Pure Crisp White) provides contrast against the cream canvas, finished with a subtle border (`rgba(24, 61, 40, 0.08)`).
- **Secondary Surface**: `#f5f4f0` (Muted Bone/Pebble) used for table headers, inactive toggle backgrounds, and secondary card containers.
- **Sage Wash**: `#eef5f0` used for trust banner cards, FAQ active items, and subtle feature backdrops.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly to establish a clean, architectural modernism. The typeface balances crisp geometric clarity with humane, slightly rounded terminal forms, reflecting landscaped precision alongside natural greenery.

- **Headlines & Display**: Rendered with tighter letter-spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) and solid weights (600 and 700) to project structural strength.
- **Section Kicker / Overline**: Styled using `label-caps` in uppercase, paired with a small circular leaf or diamond glyph in emerald green (`#2e7d45`).
- **Body & Copy**: Maintained at generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.65`) using slate tones (`#2d3732`) rather than pure pitch black, preserving high legibility against the cream background without harsh eye fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a standard **12-column fluid grid** system bounded by a max container width of `1280px`.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12 columns with `2rem` (32px) gutters and `5rem` (80px) outer section margin.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) canvas margin.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) canvas margin.

Vertical cadence follows rhythmic steps: `space-xl` (40px) between card stacks, `space-2xl` (64px) between standard content subsections, and `space-3xl` (96px) separating major landing narrative blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through layered surface tones, micro borders, and diffused ambient drop shadows tinted with botanic slate rather than artificial neutral gray.

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Elements resting flush on canvas (`#faf8f5` or `#ffffff`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Sub-element)**: Standard crisp white container cards.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(24, 61, 40, 0.07)`
  - Shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(24, 34, 28, 0.04), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(24, 34, 28, 0.06)`
- **Level 2 (Hover & Floating Controls)**: Interactive cards on hover, language switchers, and the Before/After center draggable slider handle.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(24, 61, 40, 0.12)`
  - Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(24, 34, 28, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(24, 34, 28, 0.04)`
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs)**: Quick quote slide-outs, modal drawers, and sticky bottom booking bars.
  - Shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(24, 34, 28, 0.16)`

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** core language for buttons, badges, status chips, and control handles, harmonized with generous modern radii (`1.5rem` / 24px to `2rem` / 32px) on large imagery cards and before-and-after showcase frames.

- **Buttons, Toggles, Tags & Badges**: Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Standard Cards & Form Inputs**: Moderately rounded with `1rem` (16px) corners for balanced density and content framing.
- **Hero Image Viewports & Large Featured Cards**: Deeply rounded with `1.5rem` (24px) to `2rem` (32px) corners, echoing curated garden bed borders and architectural landscape pavers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill Button with Arrow Badge**: Deep forest green background (`#183d28`), white text (`label-lg`), with right-aligned inner circular badge in emerald (`#2e7d45`) or white housing a diagonal up-right arrow (`→` or `↗`). Height is 48px to 52px; internal padding is `1.5rem` on the left and `0.5rem` on the right to frame the circular icon nicely.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: Warm cream or transparent fill with an outlined border (`1.5px solid #183d28`) and dark slate text.
- **Subtle Action Button**: Soft sage background (`#d8e8dc`) with primary forest green text (`#183d28`) for secondary calls to action within cards.

### Before & After Interactive Image Slider
- Dual image container enclosed in a `1.5rem` rounded wrapper with overflow hidden.
- Central vertical divider line in crisp white (`#ffffff`, 2px width) with a floating circular handle (44px diameter) featuring dual lateral chevron indicators (`◄ ►`).
- Soft floating pill tags in top corners: "Before" (dark neutral overlay `rgba(24, 34, 28, 0.6)`) and "After" (emerald green `#2e7d45`).

### Trust Metrics Counters
- Stacked counter groups featuring oversized numbers in `headline-lg` (`#18221c`) paired with small bold labels (`label-md` in `#2e7d45` or muted slate).
- Paired with compact social proof pills: avatar stack with overlapping 32px circular client photos and an emerald star cluster accompanied by "4.9/5 from 850+ reviews".

### Service Booking Cards
- Built on crisp white background cards with a 1px border (`rgba(24, 61, 40, 0.08)`).
- Header row with icon badge in soft sage (`#eef5f0`) containing a 20px forest green icon, followed by `title-md` and `body-sm`.
- Integrated micro-form inputs: stacked compact inputs with light neutral backgrounds (`#faf8f5`), subtle borders, and an embedded inline confirmation pill button.

### Responsive Language Toggle (EN / ES)
- Segmented pill container (`#f5f4f0`) with 4px inner padding.
- Selected state adopts a crisp white pill with Level 1 drop shadow and bold dark forest green text; unselected state displays subtle muted charcoal (`#5d6b63`).

### Accessible Accordion FAQs
- Horizontal rule dividers or isolated rounded card tiles (`#ffffff`).
- Row layout: `title-md` on the left with an expanding circular toggle button on the right (36px pill containing `+` transitioning to `−` or rotating chevron).
- Active state displays an open panel with a delicate sage background tint (`#eef5f0`) and smooth height transition.

### Review Quote Cards
- Horizontal split or stacked layout: customer portrait in a rounded square (`1rem` radius) on the side, accompanied by five emerald stars (`#2e7d45`), the testimonial in `body-md` italic/regular, and customer name + verified badge below.
